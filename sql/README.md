# Analytical Questions

## Demand

1. What hours experience the highest taxi demand?
2. How does demand vary across weekdays and weekends?
3. Which taxi zones generate the most trips?
4. How does demand vary by borough?
5. What seasonal patterns exist in taxi demand?

## Revenue

6. Which pickup zones generate the most revenue?
7. Which routes generate the most revenue?
8. Which zones have the highest revenue per trip?
9. How does revenue per mile vary geographically?
10. How does revenue change throughout the day?

## Operational Efficiency

11. Which routes have unusually long trip durations?
12. How do rush-hour trips differ from non-rush-hour trips?
13. Which locations combine high demand with high revenue efficiency?

## Customer Behavior

14. How does tipping behavior vary by trip characteristics?
15. How do payment methods relate to fares and tips?

## Predictive Analytics

16. Can hourly taxi demand be predicted for individual taxi zones?
17. Which factors are most important for predicting demand?
18. Where and when does the forecasting model make its largest errors?
